# Contribution Guidelines

Thanks for contributing. Please keep entries useful to people who actually track sleep.

## Adding an entry

- One entry per pull request when possible.
- Format: `- [Name](link) - Description ending with a period.`
- Keep descriptions to a single sentence, factual, no marketing language.
- Place entries alphabetically within their section.
- Software should be actively maintained; devices should be commercially
  available.

## What belongs here

Tools for recording, exporting or analyzing sleep data; devices with documented data access; public datasets; reference material on how sleep metrics are defined.

## What does not

Supplements, mattresses, sleep aids, general wellness apps without data export, affiliate-driven review sites, and products with no way to access your own data.

## Disclosure

If you are the author or vendor of a submitted item, say so in the pull request. Self-submissions are welcome when the item fits the scope.
